About ICISST 2026

Innovating for a Greener, Smarter Future

The International Conference on Intelligent Systems and Sustainable Technologies (ICISST 2026) is envisioned as a global platform that brings together leading academicians, scientists, researchers, industry experts, innovators, and research scholars to exchange ideas, present research findings, and discuss emerging advancements in intelligent systems and sustainable technologies. The conference aims to foster interdisciplinary collaboration and inspire innovative solutions that address contemporary technological, industrial, environmental, and societal challenges.

The conference will be held on 26–27 June 2026 at the lush green campus of Vidya Academy of Science and Technology, located at Thalakottukara P.O., Kecheri, Thrissur, Kerala. Designed as a vibrant forum for intellectual exchange, the conference will feature keynote addresses, invited talks, technical paper presentations, workshops, panel discussions, and industry interactions that encourage participants to explore transformative ideas and collaborative research opportunities.

Call for Papers

Join us in shaping the future of intelligent and sustainable technologies.

ICISST 2026 invites original research papers, review articles, case studies, and innovative project contributions from academicians, researchers, industry professionals, practitioners, and students working in the areas of intelligent systems and sustainable technologies. Authors are encouraged to submit high-quality, unpublished research that demonstrates theoretical advancements, practical applications, experimental studies, or interdisciplinary approaches addressing current and future challenges in engineering, science, and technology.

The conference welcomes contributions in diverse domains including Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Internet of Things (IoT), Intelligent Communication Systems, Robotics and Automation, Smart Computing, Cyber Security, Sustainable Energy Systems, Green Technologies, Electric Vehicles, Smart Infrastructure, Industry 4.0, Digital Transformation, and emerging technologies that contribute to sustainable development and societal progress. Selected papers of high quality may be recommended for publication in reputed journals or conference proceedings, subject to peer review and publisher guidelines.

Researchers and professionals from academia, industry, research laboratories, and startups are encouraged to participate and contribute to meaningful discussions that promote innovation, collaboration, and sustainable technological advancement.

Paper Submission Guidelines

checklist Core requirements

  • Original Work: Authors must submit original, unpublished research papers that are not under consideration elsewhere.
  • Page Limit: The manuscript must be 6 to 8 pages.
  • File Format: Submissions must be made in MS Word/PDF format; all figures/tables must be embedded, discussed in text, and properly cited.
  • Authorship: Change in authorship after acceptance not allowed.
  • Author Affiliation: Department, College/University, City, Country only (do not include post/position).
  • Similarity Index: Authors must ensure the similarity index is below 20%.

insert_chart Figures, tables & structure

  • Figures: Must be original (no snapshots/screenshots) or you must have permission to reuse.
  • Required sections: Abstract, keywords, introduction, literature review, methodology, discussion, results, conclusion, and references.
  • Equations: Must be typed using Equation Editor / MathType (no images).
  • Tables: Must be typed properly (no images).

Host City

Thrissur – Cultural Capital of Kerala

Thrissur, widely celebrated as the Cultural Capital of Kerala, is a city where tradition, art, and history blend seamlessly with modern life. Famous for its classical art forms, spiritual landmarks, and grand celebrations, Thrissur offers visitors a deep and authentic cultural experience.

Notable attractions in and around Thrissur include:

  • Vadakkumnathan Temple:A UNESCO-recognized heritage site dedicated to Lord Shiva, admired for its ancient murals and traditional Kerala temple architecture.
  • Thrissur Zoo and State Museum: Exhibits regional wildlife, history, and culture.
  • Punnathur Kotta Elephant Sanctuary: A unique elephant sanctuary offering close‑up views of trained elephants.
  • Shakthan Thampuran Palace: A restored palace museum highlighting Thrissur’s royal legacy.
  • Athirappilly Waterfalls: Often called the “Niagara of India,” located a short drive from the city.

With its welcoming atmosphere and diverse attractions, Thrissur provides an enriching cultural backdrop for conference participants.
